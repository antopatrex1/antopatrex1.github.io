That curiosity has taken me from building a company in a dorm room to space engineering, Formula 1, and post-training data for Grok at xAI. Today, it’s what drives my work at CosmicBrain AI.

I’m drawn to the place where software meets something you can touch. An idea becomes a mechanism. A model becomes motion. Something that lived in a notebook starts working in the world.

I want to build technology that advances humanity, and I like meeting people who are asking big questions and getting their hands dirty finding the answers.

A SMALL DETOUR

A little math.
A little movement.

Two links, two angles, one moving point. This is forward kinematics: a small piece of the math that connects a robot’s joints to the world.

Move the sliders and watch the arm follow. No big model needed — just geometry.

x = L₁ cos θ₁ + L₂ cos(θ₁ + θ₂)y = L₁ sin θ₁ + L₂ sin(θ₁ + θ₂)
